Before we begin

A few things
to know.

How do I enquire about a piece?

Open a collection, choose your piece and colour, then select “Ask about this piece”. Its name and colour are added to your enquiry so you can review the details before sending.

Can I compare different colours?

Yes. Use the colour options in the collection and product photographs to explore the colours shown. You can mention more than one colour in your enquiry.

Can I purchase on this website?

A.z is a curated catalogue with personal guidance. Enquire about the piece for its current price, availability and how to proceed.

What if my email app does not open?

Select “Copy enquiry” and paste your note into your preferred email app. You can write to alirezadk2020@gmail.com directly.
