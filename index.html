The practical details

Choose with
a clear next step.

How do I check price and availability?

Select the piece and colour in the catalogue, then choose “Ask about this piece”. Its details are added to your enquiry. Use the conversation with A.z to confirm the current price, colour availability and how to proceed before you decide.

Can I compare different colours?

Yes. Use the colour options in the collection and product photographs to explore the colours shown. You can mention more than one colour in your enquiry.

Does reviewing an enquiry send a message or place an order?

No. Reviewing prepares your note on this page. You can edit it, choose how to contact A.z, and send it yourself when you are ready.

Do I need an installed email app?

No. Review your enquiry to open Gmail in your browser or download a text file. You can also copy the note into your preferred email service. An installed email app is an optional route.
