## `_unused/`
Extra angles, interior and model/campaign shots you uploaded are kept in
`_unused/`. They are not shown in the grid, but nothing was deleted — you can
swap any of them into a card by pointing the matching entry in `bags.js` to it,
or ask to add an editorial layout that uses the model shots.
